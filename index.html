About

Systems-minded, five years into building the automation behind growing businesses

I'm a marketing automation specialist and project manager with over five years spent inside the tools that run modern customer journeys: email platforms, CRMs, and messaging automation. My background includes time at an Australian digital marketing agency, where I built and maintained automation for clients across e-commerce, finance, coaching, and real estate.

Today I focus on lifecycle email automation, CRM workflow design, and connecting the platforms businesses already use so leads get followed up on time, deals move through a real pipeline, and the team isn't stitching things together by hand.
